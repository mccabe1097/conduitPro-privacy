Frequently Asked Questions

What's included with ConduitPro?
Everything. ConduitPro is a one-time purchase with all features unlocked — no subscriptions, no ads, no in-app purchases. You get all 9 bend calculators, NEC conduit fill calculator, wire pull tension calculator, 60+ real bender profiles from 9 manufacturers, saved runs, export (image/PDF/text), bender calibration, training mode, home screen widgets, and reference cards.
Does the app require an internet connection?
No. ConduitPro works entirely offline. All calculations, profiles, and data are stored locally on your device.
How do I calibrate my bender?
Go to the Benders tab, select a profile, and tap "Calibrate." The 5-step wizard walks you through making a test bend and entering your measurements to fine-tune the profile for your specific bender.
Where is my data stored?
All data (saved runs, profiles, calibration records, preferences) is stored locally on your device. Nothing is sent to any server. See our Privacy Policy for details.
Is ConduitPro available for trade schools?
Yes. Educational volume pricing is available through Apple School Manager for institutions purchasing 20 or more licenses. Contact your school's IT administrator to place a volume order through Apple.
