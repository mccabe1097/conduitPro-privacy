Frequently Asked Questions

What do I get for free?
All 9 bend calculators are completely free, with no limits. The free version includes offset, 90°, back-to-back, rolling offset, 3-point saddle, 4-point saddle, kicked 90°, compound 90°, and segmented bend calculators.
What does the Pro upgrade include?
The one-time $4.99 Pro upgrade unlocks bender profiles (30+ real benders), saved runs, export (image/PDF/text), bender calibration, and training mode. No subscription — pay once, own it forever.
How do I restore my purchase on a new device?
Go to Settings (gear icon on the Calculator tab) and tap "Restore Purchases." Your Pro upgrade is tied to your Apple ID and can be restored on any device.
Does the app require an internet connection?
No. ConduitPro works entirely offline. All calculations, profiles, and data are stored locally on your device.
How do I calibrate my bender?
Go to the Benders tab, select a profile, and tap "Calibrate." The 5-step wizard walks you through making a test bend and entering your measurements to fine-tune the profile for your specific bender.
Where is my data stored?
All data (saved runs, profiles, calibration records, preferences) is stored locally on your device. Nothing is sent to any server. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Please email us at mccabe1097@gmail.com with a description of the issue or your idea. Screenshots are always helpful!
