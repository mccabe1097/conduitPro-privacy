Privacy Policy

ConduitPro — Conduit Bending Calculator for iOS

ConduitPro does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All your data stays on your device.

Data Storage

ConduitPro stores all application data locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework and UserDefaults. This includes:

  • Saved calculation runs
  • Bender profiles and calibration records
  • App preferences and settings
  • Widget data shared between the app and its home screen widgets

This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third party.

No Data Collection

ConduitPro does not collect any information from you. Specifically, the app does not use:

  • Analytics or tracking frameworks
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Crash reporting services
  • User accounts or login
  • Network requests to external servers

In-App Purchases

ConduitPro offers a one-time in-app purchase processed entirely by Apple through StoreKit. We do not receive or store any payment information. Apple's privacy policy governs all purchase transactions.

Third-Party Services

ConduitPro does not integrate any third-party SDKs, services, or APIs. The app operates entirely offline.

Children's Privacy

ConduitPro does not collect data from any user, including children under 13. The app is safe for users of all ages.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, the revised version will be posted at this URL with an updated effective date. Since the app collects no data, we do not anticipate material changes.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at the support email listed on our App Store page.
